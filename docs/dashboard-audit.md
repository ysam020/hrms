# Today dashboard audit

Audit of the Today dashboard (`/dashboard`) before turning it into an
action-oriented workspace. Everything here was traced in code on
2026-10-28. "Verified" means read in the source; "unverified" means the
code doesn't settle it. Companion documents:
[dashboard-data-inventory.md](./dashboard-data-inventory.md) (every data
point and its source) and [dashboard-role-matrix.md](./dashboard-role-matrix.md)
(capabilities and defaults).

## 3. Current problems

**Hierarchy.** Every widget is a peer on one grid. Nothing separates "you must
act" from "for your information". For an HR user, 10 sections render at equal
weight; the only decisions (Needs Your Decision, the People Ops Queue) sit
among reports. Your Day takes the first screen for everyone, including
executives who rarely punch in from the dashboard.

**Duplication.**

- Pending approvals: greeting line, Needs Your Decision, sidebar badge, and for join requests also People Ops Queue and Organisation Admin.
- Pending salary advances: People Ops Queue _and_ Payroll.
- Headcount 48: Organisation Today ("Headcount") and Workforce ("Active Employees").
- Celebrations: greeting and Coming Up.
- Team Leave Ahead pending items are also in Needs Your Decision for the same lead.

**Misleading or weak figures.**

- _Past SLA_ counts the `slaBreached` flag. A delayed BullMQ job sets it at the deadline, with a lazy fallback when tickets are listed, so it is right only while the scheduler is running; `slaDeadline < now` doesn't depend on that.
- _Hiring → applications by status_ covers every application ever, including closed roles, so "Applied 24" isn't a current pipeline.
- _Attendance Trend_ divides past days by today's headcount and counts people on approved leave as absent.
- _Headcount Trend_ drops former employees without an exit date (it says so).
- _"Not punched in yet"_ includes people whose working day hasn't started, and "today" is the UTC day — for an India-based tenant, before 05:30 IST "today" is still yesterday.
- _Organisation Today_ treats "In" as anyone with a check-in, so late arrivals are invisible even though the data records them (below).
- Pre-existing, outside the dashboard: `GET /get-all-attendances` counts only `status === "Present"`, so "Late" and "WFH" days are missing from its presents column.

**Visual.** Section hairlines plus generous gaps make the page long; KPI rows
(Metrics) appear in five widgets; two trend charts are bar-only with no
comparison; colour is mostly neutral (good) but "Pending" amber appears on
every self-request regardless of urgency.

## 6. Recommended dashboard architecture

1. **Server-side insight rules**, next to the existing widget loaders: one
   function per insight returning `{ id, category, title, reason, count,
oldest?, deadline?, priority, action: { label, to }, scope }` or nothing.
   Priority is a fixed order (overdue/past deadline → decisions waiting →
   due within the window → informational), then age. Each rule is gated by
   the same capability as its source screen and is unit-tested.
2. **One "Needs Attention" queue** at the top (Layer A+B), replacing the
   greeting's counts, the People Ops Queue and the decision duplicates.
   Approvals stay inline (approve/reject already enforced server-side).
   Insights are not stored, so there's nothing to dismiss server-side; a
   per-user "hide for today" can live in the existing preferences row if
   wanted.
3. **Context** (Layer C): Your Day (compact for executives), Team Today,
   Organisation Today, leave, requests — one number in one place.
4. **Insights** (Layer D): trends and breakdowns lower on the page, collapsed
   by default for operational roles, with links to `/analytics/*`.
5. Keep the current widget registry, preferences and Customize; the attention
   queue is always first and can't be hidden (hiding obligations is out of
   scope), but its categories follow the same capabilities.

## 7. Material blockers

- **Time zone.** "Today", lateness and "due today" all depend on a tenant time zone Praesto doesn't store. Building lateness or day-based exceptions on server-local time risks wrong flags. Needs a decision (§8).
- **No browser test accounts.** Role screenshots so far used stubbed APIs. Verifying against real data needs seeded tenants or accounts.

## 8. Open questions

1. What time zone do production services run in, and should tenants get a configurable time zone before lateness or "today" rules ship?
2. What counts as "approaching" an SLA, "stalled" in hiring, and "old" for KYC — fixed defaults (e.g. 4 h, 7 days, 3 days) or tenant settings?
3. Should team leads see team members' lateness, or only HR? (The data exists; the permission catalog has `Attendance:view:team`, which nothing enforces today.)
4. Should `Department.headUserId` grant department-wide visibility? Today it grants nothing.
5. Do executives want Your Day on their dashboard at all?
6. Is the pre-existing `get-all-attendances` "presents excludes Late/WFH" behaviour intended (payroll may depend on it)?
7. Should approval items show age only, or is an approval SLA wanted (it would need a new setting)?
